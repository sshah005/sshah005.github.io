## Selected Projects

### African American Obstacles in Education
Investigated racial disparities in educational outcomes, with emphasis on STEM.
The project examined structural inequities and developed policy-oriented
recommendations involving equitable funding, mentorship, and culturally
responsive teaching.

### Norfolk Wellness and Activity Program (NWAP)
Designed a wellness initiative for eighth-grade students in Norfolk focused on
physical activity, nutrition education, behavioral outcomes, and equity.

### Service Learning Project on Financial Review
Analyzed five years of Form 990 data to assess financial trends and resource
allocation in a nonprofit context and proposed strategies to improve
transparency and long-term sustainability.
